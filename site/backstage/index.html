Backstage

The crew's control room. The Ringleaders run game night polls here, stamp Game Night passes, keep the Hall of Oddities clean and check that the Belpire Usher can do its job.

Checking your pass…

Before you pull any levers

  • Who gets in. Only the Ringleaders: the RingMaster, RingMistress, Carnival Guards and the Fortune Teller. The site checks your Discord role on the server before anything you do back here, so adding or removing a role takes effect within a couple of minutes.
  • Some buttons talk out loud. Closing a poll and setting its date post to #general and create the event in The Main Stage. Stamping or turning away a pass sends the member a DM from the Belpire Usher.
  • Everything else stays back here. Members never see this page, the queues or who decided what. The Green Room is mirrored only to the Moderation Tent, which members can't see either.
  • Look before you leap. Close and Cancel need a second click to confirm. Removing an oddity deletes the file for good.

Act I

Game night polls

Open one poll for each upcoming game night. Up to eight can run at once, each with its own ballots, so you can line up a whole series. When voting closes, the top four are posted to #general; then set the date to create the event in The Main Stage.

Act II

Game Night passes

The Game Night role is the key to The Main Stage. Requests come from the button on The Big Tent or from a Yes in Discord onboarding. Stamp it gives the role and DMs a welcome; Turn away closes the request, and any reason you type goes to them by DM.

Act III

Hall of Oddities review

Uploads that need a human eye: pictures the safety scan wasn't sure about, every clip from members, and anything three members reported. Approve hangs it in the hall; Remove deletes it. Game and horror gore is fine; nudity, real-world gore, hate symbols and personal details are not. Ringleaders' own uploads skip this line.

Act IV

The Prize Booth

Members earn tickets from the daily punch card, Find the Pea and checking in at shows, and spend them here. The tickets are already taken when an order lands, so your job is to hand over the prize and press Mark done (the Usher DMs them), or Refund to give the tickets back. "Put a game on the ballot" ticks itself off when you open a poll with that game; it's pre-ticked in the poll form above. You can also hand out tickets for great moments.

Act V

The Green Room

The crew's thread: hand-offs, heads-ups and "who's running tonight". Every Ringleader sees it here, and the Belpire Usher posts each message to the Moderation Tent in Discord so nobody misses it. Messages are kept for 90 days; you can delete your own, here and in Discord.
