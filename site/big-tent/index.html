    How the votes are counted

    Your first pick earns a game 4 points, your second 3, your third 2 and your fourth 1. You can rank just one game if that's all you want to play. Ties go to the game with more first-place votes. You can change your ballot until voting closes, and nobody sees the standings until then.

    Feeling daring? The Wheel of Doom spins four times over the horror, party and Jackbox games on the ballot and fills in your vote for you. Its picks count exactly like a normal ballot, but they lock in. No take-backs.
