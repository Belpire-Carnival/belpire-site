Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026

belpire.com is the website of the Belpire Carnival, a community for multiplayer horror and party games that lives on Discord. This policy explains what happens to your information when you visit the site. The short version: we don't ask for anything, we don't track you, and we don't sell or share data.

What we collect

Nothing you type in. The site has no accounts, sign-up forms, comments or newsletters.

No cookies, analytics or tracking. The site sets no cookies, uses no analytics or advertising tools, and stores nothing in your browser.

Technical data needed to run a website. When your browser requests a page, the services below necessarily receive standard request information such as your IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time. We don't combine this with anything else or use it to identify you.

Services that handle requests

  • Cloudflare hosts the site and protects it from attacks. It processes request data to deliver pages and keep the site secure. Our server code keeps short-lived error and activity logs through Cloudflare, retained only for Cloudflare's standard short period, so we can fix problems. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts serves the site's typefaces, so your browser requests them from Google. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Steam (Valve) serves the game artwork shown on the homepage, so your browser loads those images from Steam's servers. See Valve's privacy policy.
  • GitHub hosts the site's source code, which is public. Visiting belpire.com doesn't send anything to GitHub.

Game news, Star Citizen server status and tool versions are fetched by our server from Steam, Roberts Space Industries and GitHub. Those requests come from the site, not from your browser, and contain no information about you.

Links to other sites

The site links to Discord, Steam, Roberts Space Industries and community tools. Once you follow a link, that site's own privacy policy applies. Joining our Discord server is covered by Discord's privacy policy and our server rules.

Children

The site isn't directed to children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone. Our Discord community follows Discord's own minimum age requirements.

Your choices and rights

Because we don't keep personal information about visitors, there is normally nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you think we hold something about you, or you have a question about privacy, contact us and we'll help. Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the GDPR or US state privacy laws; we'll honor them.

Changes

If the site starts collecting anything new, for example if we add login with Discord, we'll update this page before that feature goes live and change the date at the top.

Contact

Email ringmaster@belpire.com, or message a Ringleader in the Belpire Discord.
