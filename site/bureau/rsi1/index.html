03 Field notes

The theatre

How DIV_RSI1 runs a contract, and how to get on the next one. The creed and rules of engagement are at Bureau HQ.

On a contract

  • Every op has a Squad Leader. Their call is final until the contract closes.
  • Pilots, haulers, medics and boots on the ground count the same.
  • Heavy freight rides with escorts; nobody hauls alone into a contested lane.
  • Interdicted or contested at the objective? Answer with overwhelming force, then back to the mission.
  • Payouts and salvage are split fairly across everyone who flew.

Reporting in

  1. Get an RSI account. A free Roberts Space Industries account is all it takes.
  2. Join FINTAC. Through Spectrum (opens a new tab) or the org page (opens a new tab). Casual commitment, PvE first.
  3. Find your squad. Ops are called in the Bureau Discord (opens a new tab).
