02 Field notes

The theatre

How the Bureau’s creed plays out in the ’verse.

On a contract

  • Every op has a Squad Leader. Their call is final until the contract closes.
  • Pilots, haulers, medics and boots on the ground count the same.
  • Heavy freight rides with escorts; nobody hauls alone into a contested lane.
  • Payouts and salvage are split fairly across everyone who flew.

Rules in the ’verse

  • No piracy, no griefing, no unprovoked PvP. Not even for an easy score.
  • Interdicted, fired on or contested at the objective? Answer with overwhelming force.
  • No looting a site without the go-ahead.
  • Threat cleared, back to the mission.

The full creed, chain of command and history live at Bureau HQ.
