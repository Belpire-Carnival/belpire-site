      The loop: hull-scrape wrecks into Recycled Material Composite (RMC), then optionally fracture and disintegrate the hull into raw construction material. Since Alpha 4.3.2 that raw material must be refined into Construction Materials before it sells. The grade you get depends on ship class: Rubble (light salvage: fastest refining, lowest yield), Pieces, then Salvage.

      Sources: RSI Salvage & Repair guide (Comm-Link 19070), starcitizen.tools Salvage, timesaver.gg 4.10 guide.

        Don't misread Admin offices: many only sell RMC to you (seen at 7,587). That's a buy price, not a payout.
        • RMC: Trade & Development Division (TDD) offices and scrapyards; some commodity terminals.
        • Construction Materials: in demand at admin offices (refine first).
        • Prices drift by shard and stock. Check UEX commodities before the trip.

        Contracts & mitigations

        • Salvager Needed orders (Adagio Holdings) ask for RMC, unrefined construction material (Rubble, Pieces or Salvage) and components, delivered to a freight elevator (e.g. Levski). Any class or size of salvage ship qualifies. Seen on 4.10.2.
        • Contract bonuses can pay out if a wreck is "sufficiently salvaged" or towed far enough from its spawn. A timer eventually brings security forces.
        • Scan before you scrape. Owner "Gamerules" means abandoned. Scraping a ship that still shows a player name can flag you for a crime.
        • Shields or power still on? They block scraping. Knock the ship down with distortion damage first.
        • Eject RMC before structural salvage. Construction material uses the same filler buffer.
        • Sell before you log off. Loose cargo and refinery jobs can be lost to a crash or version change. LTP protects aUEC and in-game-earned ships, not loose RMC in your grid.
        • Error 30000: fully close the client before reconnecting.

        Refinery job calculator

        One line per ore. Yield % and fee come from the refinery terminal's estimate for the method you pick. Type an ore name from the list, then "Fill sell prices" uses the best live sell price. Edit any number.

        Ore / materialRaw SCUYield %Sell / SCU

        Picking the platform

        • Prospector: solo ship mining. Choose laser head and modules for the rock's resistance and instability.
        • MOLE: crewed ship mining with three heads that can work one rock together.
        • ROC / Golem / hand mining: small surface gems, often in caves.
        • Modules stack. Active modules trade charge-window stability for power, so plan the stack per rock rather than one fit for all.
        Best ore and refinery yield bonuses rotate. Before a session, check Regolith for rock and refinery data and UEX for refined prices.

        Mitigations

        • Refinery jobs can be lost to version changes. Collect and sell finished jobs before a patch lands.
        • Fracture on a stable charge window; if the bar runs away, cut power rather than riding the overcharge.

        Best buy-to-sell pairs across all terminals, from recent UEX reports (older than two weeks is ignored). "Buy" is what you pay, "Sell" is what you get. Margins this good deserve a second look: check in game before you commit a hold.

        PvP risk comes from how each place is set up: the wiki says whether it is an Armistice Zone (Safe, or Medium or worse if not), plus a rule of thumb for contested zones and lawless systems. It is not live: nothing tells us who is camping where. Each run shows the buy end, the sell end, and a run score out of 10 (Safe 0, Low 1, Unknown 2, Medium 3, High 5 per end).

        Mitigations

        • Use the Cargo Grid tab to plan boxes before you hit the freight elevator.
        • Freight elevator stuck or empty: re-open the terminal, switch tabs, and wait for the elevator to cycle before re-requesting. Don't spam the request.
        • Store cargo you mean to keep. Don't log off with a full hold you haven't sold or stored.
        • Check SC Trade Tools for current route ideas, then price-check them here.
