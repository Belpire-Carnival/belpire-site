01 The crest

The creed on the crest

Every mark on the Bureau’s seal stands for something, in every division and every game. Balanced on the scales, held up by the sword, under one star.

The Admiral’s Will

The star at the top. Command sets the course, and on a live contract the call is final.

Sacrificium

The Martyr’s Sacrifice

The unit before the self. Cover the squad, hold the line, carry the wounded out.

Accuratia

The Sheriff’s Precision

Every shot accounted for. Confirm the target, hit what you aim at, nothing else.

Strategy

The plan comes before the fight. Brief it, fly it, hold formation.

Objective

The mission is the point. Ego gets people killed, so the objective comes first.

Demolition

When it has to come down, it comes down. Objectives cleared and sealed.

Eliminatio

The book closes on every contract. Threats end; the Bureau goes home.

03 History

The dossier

  1. 2184

    Founded

    Raised as a precision unit and hardened in the sieges of Malevelon Creek and Hellmire, the front DIV_SE1 still holds. The Old Guard learned the rule the unit still runs on: ego gets people killed.

  2. 2186

    The jump that failed

    An FTL jump went wrong. The command vessel dropped out of contact and the whole crew went under in cryo-stasis aboard a drifting ghost ship.

  3. +750 yrs

    The long drift

    Centuries passed. Empires rose. Nobody came looking.

  4. 2945

    Salvaged in Stanton

    Salvagers cracked the pods open in a system run by corporations and outlaws. The Bureau woke, took stock and stood up its first new division there: DIV_RSI1.

  5. Now

    Every front

    No empire-building, no glory-seeking. Wherever the Bureau deploys, clients get the job done and operatives share the payout.

Discipline over ego. The unit over the individual.

04 The charter

Rules of engagement

These hold in every division.

When we fire

  • No unprovoked hostilities, piracy, griefing or PvP.
  • Fired on, or a hostile contests the objective? Answer with overwhelming force.
  • Threat cleared, back to the mission.

How we operate

  • Follow the plan and hold formation.
  • No looting without the go-ahead.
  • Comms stay clear and professional.
  • Toxicity and insubordination mean review and discharge.

Survival requires discipline. Victory requires focus.

05 Chain of command

How it works

  • One chain of command across every division.
  • Rank is earned by showing up and being reliable, not handed out.
  • On a live contract, the Squad Leader’s call is final.
  • Every role counts the same, and payouts are split fairly across everyone who deployed.
