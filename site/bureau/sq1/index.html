01 Access

Cleared personnel only

The TCC isn’t a game division. It’s the command net every division runs on, and it’s where members get what the public site doesn’t show.

Who gets in

  • Anyone who joins the Discord and accepts the rules. Allied operatives from other orgs are welcome too.
  • Pick your divisions when you report in; their channels open to you.
  • Rank is earned in the field: Recruit, Private, Sergeant, Warrant Officer, Admiral, Commander.

Standing orders

  • The same creed and rules of engagement as every division.
  • What’s said in the TCC stays in the TCC.
  • Toxicity and insubordination mean review and discharge.
